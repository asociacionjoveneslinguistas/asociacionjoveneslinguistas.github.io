The Association of Young Linguists was founded in Barcelona (Spain) in 1992. It comprises researchers in training, predoctoral researchers, and generally, members of the faculty of Philology and others dedicated to the study of languages.

We organize an annual international congress at a Spanish University, celebrated each year at a different location.
